```text
🚀 HUGE UPDATE: 32× Faster Read-Along .MP4 Video Exporter + 100% Word-for-Word Sync & Zero-Clutter EPUB Cleaner (Codecaine File Manager v1.5.3) 🎙️🎬📚

We just dropped a massive engineering update to the built-in Phonon Voice Reader Studio inside Codecaine File Manager — and the entire walkthrough video is narrated 100% live and offline on Mac using ollama run phonon --voice Russell!

Here is everything we engineered in v1.5.3 and why it matters:

⚡ 1. 32× Real-Time Read-Along .MP4 Video Exporter (In-Process Bun FFI + Native Cairo & Pango)
Previously, rendering frame-by-frame Read-Along videos meant spawning heavy filter graphs. We replaced that with an in-process native Cairo + Pango renderer via bun:ffi across macOS (.dylib), Linux (.so), and Windows (.dll):
• Level 1 (baseBg): Renders the radial vignette, top title pill, and progress track ONCE per video.
• Level 2 (cueBg): Renders the centered cinema card and inactive words ONCE per sentence cue.
• Level 3 (Word Delta Slice): Copies only the narrow horizontal slice of the active line, draws the glowing cyan pill (#38bdf8) around the active word, and streams raw BGRA frames into multi-core parallel hardware H.264 shards (VideoToolbox / libx264).
👉 Result: A 2m 33s 1080p Read-Along MP4 + companion .SRT and .VTT subtitle files now exports in just 4.79 seconds (32× faster than real-time)!

🎯 2. 100% Word-for-Word Audio-to-Text Sync (Zero Skipped Words)
Have you ever noticed read-along players skipping short words like "I", "on", "a", or "to"? Here is how we fixed it:
• First-Difference Pre-Emphasis (y[n] = x[n] - 0.95*x[n-1]): Boosts high-frequency consonant and sibilant energy (s, t, k, f) in the 24 kHz PCM waveform so word boundaries are razor-sharp instead of blurred by vowel tails.
• Intra-Sentence Comma & Colon Valley Snapping: Snaps both sentence endings AND internal commas, semicolons, and em-dashes directly to real acoustic silence valleys in the voice recording.
• All-Caps Heading vs. Acronym Disambiguation: Distinguishes normal uppercase book headings (BOOK, ONE, CHAPTER) from true spelled-out initialisms (MP4, SRT, VTT, CPU, GPU, API).
• 40 ms Visual Hold Floor + 60 FPS Teleprompter Loop: Guarantees every single word gets at least one full video frame on screen and updates the browser teleprompter via requestAnimationFrame so zero words are ever skipped!

🧹 3. Zero Conversion Clutter (Calibre / Pandoc EPUB & Speech Sanitizer)
Converting e-books often leaves behind thousands of broken artifacts that ruin text-to-speech. Our new sanitizer automatically strips:
• Broken Calibre & Pandoc markup: {.calibre*}, ::: fenced divs, []{#id} empty anchors, inline SVG blocks, escaped quotes \', and stray backslashes (while keeping Windows C:\... paths intact!).
• Non-speech ASCII dividers: Rows of ======, ------, ******, and box-drawing lines so your narrator never reads "equals equals equals" aloud.
• Smart Back-Matter Promo Filter: Scans the final 45% of an e-book and automatically removes appended "Sneak Peek", "Preview of Book", and "Also by Author" promo chapters while preserving every genuine Author's Note, Foreword, and Introduction!

🌍 4. Free & Open Source Across All Platforms (v1.5.3)
```
