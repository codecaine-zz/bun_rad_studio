## 1. Overview

**Codecaine Mr. Game Show Royale: TV Studio Mega-Pack** (`applications/game_show_studio.ts`, Port `3277`) is an 8-in-1 primetime TV game show broadcast studio hosted live on stage by **🎩 Mr. Caine-Show** alongside two AI studio champions (`🤖 LEXI-9000` and `🎰 MAX-VEGAS`).

### Quick Launch
```bash
bun run app:game_show
```

## 2. Eight Primetime TV Game Show Modes (`#showModeSelect` / Keys `1`–`8`)

1. **🎡 Wheel of Fortune & Phrase Board (`wheel_phrase`)**: Spin the 24-wedge 3D Cash Wheel (`$500` to `$10,000`, `FREE SPIN`, `BANKRUPT`), call consonants, buy vowels (`$250`), or solve the illuminated 3-row Crossword Phrase Board for a `+$5,000` solve bonus.
2. **👹 Press Your Luck: No Whammies Big Board (`press_luck`)**: Watch the high-speed perimeter light bounce across 16 animated prize squares and slam **`🛑 STOP BIG BOARD! [SPACE]`** to claim Big Bucks (`$1,250`–`$10,000`, `2× Bank`) while dodging the **👹 Cyber-Whammy**.
3. **📋 Cyber-Feud: Survey Says Top 6! (`survey_feud`)**: Flip the Top 6 survey answers on the classic LED studio board.
4. **🧠 Cyber-Jeopardy! 5×5 Grid (`jeopardy_grid`)**: Select clues across 5 categories (`$200`–`$1,000`) and buzz in (`[SPACE]`) ahead of your AI rivals.
5. **🪙 Price Is Right: Plinko $50,000 Drop (`plinko_price`)**: Release Gold Plinko discs down a 9-row peg pyramid toward the `$10,000` center slot.
6. **💼 Deal or No Deal: 20 Golden Briefcases (`deal_cases`)**: Eliminate briefcases from `$1` to `$1,000,000` and negotiate expected-value cash buyout offers with **The Banker**.
7. **💎 Millionaire 15-Rung Ladder (`millionaire`)**: Climb from `$100` to `$1,000,000` using **4 Studio Lifelines** (`50:50`, `Poll Audience`, `Phone AI Pro`, `Whammy Shield`).
8. **👑 Mr. Game Show 4-Round TV Episode (`grand_finale`)**: Plays a continuous 4-round championship broadcast across Survey Feud, Wheel & Phrase, No Whammies, and Plinko.
