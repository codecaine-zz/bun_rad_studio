## 🌟 Executive Overview
**Codecaine CyberPipe Flow Deluxe** is an industrial-grade 8-mode cyberpunk pipe-engineering suite built natively for Bun RAD Studio. Combining the high-stakes pressure of 1989 *Pipe Mania / Pipe Dream* with modern puzzle-engineering mechanics, it features **8 distinct game modes**, a full **Engineer Toolkit Bar** (**Hold Bay `[H]`**, **Rotate Next Pipe `[R]`**, **Cryo-Freeze `[C]`**, **Instant Wrench `[X]`**, and **Surge Flow `[F]`**), **3D Brushed-Titanium & Copper Cylindrical Conduits** with bolted flanges and sight-windows, **Pressurized Bioluminescent Fluid Shaders** with rising micro-bubbles and an analog **PSI Pressure Gauge**, and a real-time **Hover Ghost Preview (`✔ MATCH` / `⚠ OPEN`)**.

## 🎮 Complete Guide to All 8 Engineering Modes (`#modeSelect` / Keys `1`–`8`)

### 1. 🟢 Classic 1989 Pipe Mania — Quota + Exit Drain (`classic` / `[1]`)
- **Objective**: Build a continuous conduit from the Starter Pump (`🟢 PUMP`) across the `10×7` matrix to meet the sector's pipe distance quota (`targetDist`) — or link directly into the **Exit Drain (`🎯 DRAIN`)** for an instant **+5,000 PTS Drain Bonus**!

### 2. 🔄 Plumber's Rotate-All Logic Grid — 100 Solvable Levels (`plumber_rotate` / `[2]`)
- **Objective**: Every cell on the `10×7` grid starts pre-filled with scrambled pipe segments generated by a deterministic solvable path algorithm.
- **Mechanic**: Click any cell (or press `Space` / `R`) to **rotate that pipe 90° clockwise** in place! Connected pipes from the Source Pump illuminate in real time; link the circuit into the **Exit Drain (`🎯 DRAIN`)** and press **`[F]`** to test flow!

### 3. ⚗️ Chromatic Dual-Stream Mixing — Cyan + Gold → Emerald (`dual_color` / `[3]`)
- **Objective**: Two starter pumps (**🔵 Cyan Coolant** and **🟡 Gold Coolant**) pump simultaneously!
- **Mechanic**: Route both streams across the grid to the Exit Drains, or merge them through the central **Chromatic Mixer Chamber (`⚗️`)** to synthesize **🟢 Emerald Hyper-Coolant (`+750 PTS`)**!

### 4. ☢️ Reactor Meltdown Rescue — 4 Core Vents (`reactor_boss` / `[4]`)
- **Objective**: A central **Nuclear Cyber-Reactor (`☢️`)** is overheating toward critical meltdown (`420°C → 1,200°C`).
- **Mechanic**: Route your pressurized coolant stream through all **4 Reactor Cooling Vents (`❄️ V1–V4`)** on the board. Each cooled vent drops core temperature by `250°C` and awards **+1,000 PTS**!

### 5. 🚀 Master Arcade — Reservoirs, Boosters, Check Valves & Omni-Hub (`bomb_arcade` / `[5]`)
- **Objective**: High-voltage arcade mode featuring advanced industrial hardware in the dispenser:
  - **`⏳ Reservoir Tank`**: Holds 4× fluid volume, stalling coolant for **4 full seconds** (`+400 PTS`).
  - **`🚀 Booster Valve`**: Accelerates fluid velocity + awards **`+350 PTS`** turbo bonus.
  - **`▶ One-Way Check Valve`**: Directional check valve rotatable via **`[R]`**.
  - **`🌈 Omni-Hub`**: Universal 4-way manifold that automatically routes fluid toward connected ports (`+500 PTS`).

### 6. 💎 Budget Blueprint Architect — Engineering Economy (`architect` / `[6]`)
- **Objective**: Manage a **`$2,500` Engineering Credit Budget** where every placed conduit deducts credits (`Straight $50`, `Elbow $75`, `Cross $150`, `Omni $200`).
- **Mechanic**: Route your pipeline through **Bonus Gem Veins (`💎 +$400`)** and into the **Exit Drain** to maximize your Net Profit!

### 7. 🧘 Zen Endless Flow — Free Rotate + Manual Release (`zen` / `[7]`)
- **Objective**: Zero countdown stress! Coolant never flows until you press **`⏩ Surge Flow [F]`**. Clicking any placed dry pipe rotates it `90°` in place with zero jackhammer delay, and **Instant Wrench (`[X]`)** is unlimited.

### 8. 🤝 2-Plumber Co-Op / Versus Grid (`versus_2p` / `[8]`)
- **Objective**: Dual cursors and dual simultaneous starter pumps! **Plumber 1 (`P1 Cyan`: `WASD + Space`, `Q` Rotate)** and **Plumber 2 (`P2 Gold`: `Mouse` or `Arrows + Enter`)** build pipelines side-by-side.

## 🧰 Modern Engineer Toolkit Bar

| Tool | Shortcut / Button | Engineering Function |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Hold Pipe Slot** | `H` / `📦 Hold [H]` | Swap the active bottom pipe in the 5-piece dispenser with your dedicated **Hold Bay** (like Tetris Hold) to save a `╋` Cross or `🌈` Omni-Hub. |
| **Rotate Next Pipe** | `R` / `Right-Click` / `🔄 Rotate [R]` | Rotate the active pipe at the bottom of the dispenser queue `90°` clockwise before placing it (or rotate pre-filled pipes in `Rotate-All` & `Zen`). |
| **Cryo-Freeze Flow** | `C` / `❄️ Cryo [C]` | Freezes all advancing coolant streams for **`5.0 seconds`** (`+1` charge every 6 pipes flowed). |
| **Instant Wrench** | `X` / `Backspace` / `🧨 Wrench [X]` | Cleanly removes a misplaced dry pipe at your cursor **instantly** without jackhammer lockout! |
| **Surge Flow (5×)** | `F` / `⏩ Surge Flow [F]` | Accelerates coolant flow `5×` once your conduit circuit is complete (or releases flow in `Zen`). |

## 🐧 Slow Linux Machines — Brave & Chrome Browser Performance Tip
- On slower Linux machines, integrated GPUs, or virtual machines where the native `libwebkitgtk-6.0-4` window uses software rendering, **Brave**, **Google Chrome**, and other Chromium-based browsers run Canvas 2D graphics, particle effects, and Web Audio synthesizers significantly faster.
- Launch with `--server` (or start Codecaine File Manager with `bun run app:files --server`) and open the printed `http://127.0.0.1:<port>` URL in **Brave** or **Chrome** for smooth 60fps gameplay.
- Press **`Alt+G`** at any time to open the **Game Master FX HUD** and toggle off **CRT Scanlines** or **Cabinet Glow** to further reduce GPU/CPU compositing overhead on older hardware.
