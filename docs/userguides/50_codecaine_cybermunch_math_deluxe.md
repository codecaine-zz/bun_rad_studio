## 3. All 8 Game Modes (`1–8` Hotkeys & Top Pill Bar)

Switch modes before starting or **live mid-game** via the top mode pill bar or number keys `1`–`8`:

1. **🔢 Mode 1: Classic Number Munchers (`classic`)**  
   Cycles through the 5 iconic MECC mathematical rules—**Multiples**, **Factors**, **Primes**, **Equalities**, and **Inequalities**—or adapts directly to your chosen Curriculum Tier.
2. **🍕 Mode 2: Fraction, Decimal & Percent Munchers (`fractions`)**  
   Inspired by *Fraction Munchers*! Match equivalent fractions (`1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 = 4/8`), decimals (`0.25`, `0.5`, `0.75`), and percentages (`25%`, `50%`, `75%`).
3. **🌍 Mode 3: Super Munchers STEM & Fact Categorization (`super_trivia`)**  
   Inspired by *Super Munchers*! Chomp **Perfect Squares (`n²`)**, **Fibonacci Sequence Numbers (`1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21...`)**, and **Geometry Angle Sums (`360°`, `2π rad`)**.
4. **📐 Mode 4: Algebra & Equation Munchers (`algebra`)**  
   Solve linear, exponential, and quadratic equations on the fly for target roots (`x = 5`, `x = 3`) with step-by-step algebraic isolation proofs.
5. **∫ Mode 5: Trigonometry & AP Calculus Munchers (`advanced_stem`)**  
   Evaluate Unit Circle identities (`sin²θ + cos²θ = 1`), natural logarithms (`ln(e)`), Power Rule derivatives (`d/dx[x²] = 2x`), and definite integrals (`∫₀² 2x dx = 4`).
6. **💻 Mode 6: Computer Science & Binary Hacker (`cs_hacker`)**  
   Convert between Binary (`0b1000`), Hexadecimal (`0x0F`), Bitwise shifts (`1 << 3`), XOR/AND masks (`0xFF & 15`), and Modulo remainders (`31 % 16`).
7. **👾 Mode 7: Mega-Troggle Boss Raid (`boss_raid`)**  
   The **Mega-Troggle Overlord** hovers atop the grid with a live Boss HP bar! Every valid math chomp fires a **Plasma Math Bolt** (`-12 Boss HP`), and clearing the board unleashes an **Orbital Strike** (`-35 Boss HP` + `+2,000` defeat bonus).
8. **👥 Mode 8: 2P Co-Op & Versus Math Squad (`coop_2p`)**  
   Local 2-player simultaneous grid action! **Player 1 (Emerald Muncher)** moves with `WASD` + `Space`, while **Player 2 (Cyber Cyan Muncher)** moves with `Arrow Keys` + `Enter`, with live `P1 vs P2` score telemetry rendered on canvas.

## 4. Interactive Math Tutor & Tactical Gadgets

### Live Math Tutor & Proof Strip (`#tutorProofStrip`)
- **On Every Valid Munch (`PROOF ✓`):** Displays the exact mathematical proof explaining why the chomped cell satisfies the active rule (e.g., `3/6 simplifies to 1/2 (3 ÷ 3 = 1, 6 ÷ 3 = 2)` or `Power Rule: d/dx[x³] = 3x²`).
- **On Every Mistake (`MISTAKE ✗`):** Displays a step-by-step diagnostic breakdown of why the cell was wrong (e.g., `2x = 12 gives x = 6, not 5`).
- **On Demand (`💡 TUTOR [H]`):** Highlights a valid target cell on the 6×5 board with a pulsing golden beacon AND prints its worked mathematical solution in the Tutor banner.

### Tactical Muncher Gadgets (Streak-Powered)
Every **5 consecutive valid munches** awards `+1` tactical gadget charge:
- **❄️ Cryo-Freeze (`[F]` Key / Button):** Freezes all active Troggles in place for **6.0 seconds**.
- **💡 Math Tutor (`[H]` Key / Button):** Highlights a valid cell and shows its step-by-step proof.
- **⚡ EMP Zap Troggle (`[Q]` Key / Button):** Fires a targeted EMP bolt that vaporizes the nearest Troggle off the board (`+150 pts`).
