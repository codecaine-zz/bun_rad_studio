Doubles (Standard)

Two cards of the exact same rank played consecutively (e.g. 7 on 7, K on K).

Sandwich (Standard)

Two cards of the same rank separated by exactly one card (e.g. 9, 3, 9 or Q, 5, Q).

Tens (Sum to 10)

Two consecutive cards whose numeric ranks sum to 10 (e.g. 4 and 6, Ace (1) and 9).

Marriage (King & Queen)

A King and Queen played immediately adjacent to each other in any order.

Top-Bottom

The top card matches the rank of the very first card placed at the bottom of the current pot.

Four-in-a-Row (Sequence Run)

Four cards in continuous ascending or descending sequence (e.g. 5, 6, 7, 8 or K, Q, J, 10).
