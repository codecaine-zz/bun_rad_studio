🃏 Codecaine UNO Royale — 8-Mode Manual
8 Playable UNO Modes (Switch via Top Ribbon or Keys 1–8):
• 1. 🏆 Classic 500-Pt Royale: Championship UNO with Stackable `+2/+4`, `7-0` Hand Swap/Rotate, `UNO!` Shout (`[U]`), and `Catch AI` (`[C]`).
• 2. 🌗 UNO FLIP! (Light & Dark Side): Play a 🔄 FLIP card to flip the arena between the ☀️ Light Side and the brutal 🌑 Dark Side (`+5 Draw Five`, `⏭️ Skip All`, and `🌈 Wild +6`)!
• 3. 💀 Show 'Em No Mercy (25-Card Knockout): Adds `+4`, `+6`, `Wild +10`, `⏭️ Skip All`, and `🗑️ Discard All` (`PURGE`). Any player reaching 25+ cards is ELIMINATED (`💀 KO`)!
• 4. ⚡ Jump-In & Speed Slap Blitz: Even during an AI's turn, click an identical twin card (same color & value as top discard) to shout ⚡ JUMP IN! and hijack the turn!
• 5. 🤝 2v2 Partner Team Royale: Team up with North AI (NOVA 🤝 Partner)! Share points and toggle 👁️ Peek Partner (`[P]`) to view your partner's cards face-up.
• 6. 🎰 UNO All Wild!: Every card is a Wild action card (`Wild`, `Wild Reverse`, `Wild Skip`, `Wild Skip All`, `Wild +2`, `Wild +4`, `Wild Swap`, `Wild Tornado`)!
• 7. 💣 Cyber Roulette & Time-Bomb Press: Drawing presses the Cyber Roulette bomb (`15% → 90%`) — escape with `0` cards (`SAFE!`) or trigger a `2–6` card blast!
• 8. 💻 Codecaine Cyber-Hack Deck: Adds 🗑️ Purge Color (`PURGE`), 🪞 Mirror Shield (`MIRROR` reflects draw stacks), 🌪️ Tornado Equalizer (`TORNADO` redistributes all hands evenly), and 👁️ X-Ray Reveal (`XRAY` exposes AI hands for 3 turns)!
