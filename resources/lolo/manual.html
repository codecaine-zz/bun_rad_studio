CORE DIRECTIVES & MISSION RULES

1. Collect All Heart Framers

Memory Core Extraction

Every chamber contains red glowing Heart Framers. You must collect every single Heart Framer to unlock the central chamber chest. Some Heart Framers contain compressed plasma ammo batteries that grant you 2 stasis blaster shots!

2. Decrypt the Jewel Chest

Quantum Key Recovery

Once all Heart Framers are secured, the sealed Jewel Chest opens and emits a radiant golden pulse. Stepping onto the Jewel Chest retrieves the extraction key, instantly putting all active hostiles into a deactivated stasis state and opening the exit gate.

3. Evacuate via Warp Gate

Chamber Completion

After taking the Jewel Chest, navigate to the open chamber door (located along the upper perimeter). Stepping through the doorway triggers an orbital teleportation beam, rewarding score points and advancing you to the next sector operation.

4. Egg Raft Sailing Physics

Liquid Moat Navigation

Pushing an encased enemy egg into water or molten magma creates a buoyant floating raft. Step onto the egg to sail across liquid currents! Caution: water currents will carry the raft downstream, and eggs gradually sink after a short period.

HOSTILE THREAT INTELLIGENCE DOSSIER (BESTIARY)

SNAKEY THREAT: 1/5

Reconnaissance Viper Drone

Completely passive stationary hostile. Does not move or attack. Primary role in chamber tactics is serving as an egg shield to block line-of-sight laser turrets or being pushed into liquid moats as a floating ferry raft.

Countermeasure: 1 shot = egg raft/cover. 2 shots = clear path.
GOL THREAT: 3/5

Thermobaric Plasma Hydra

Remains dormant until the final Heart Framer is collected. Once all hearts are taken, Gol opens its eyes and breathes fatal fireballs whenever Lolo crosses its cardinal line-of-sight corridor.

Countermeasure: Encase into egg before final heart, or place block in front.
SKULL THREAT: 4/5

Nanite Cyber Sentry

Stationary bone-pile sentry during initial phase. Instantly awakens upon collecting the last Heart Framer, aggressively hunting Lolo at high speed using shortest-path tracking.

Countermeasure: Stasis encasement or trap behind blocks before final heart.
ALMA THREAT: 4/5

Rolling Kinetic Juggernaut

Active patrol bot that roams corridors continuously. When it detects Lolo along an aligned axis, it morphs into a high-speed bowling ball and charges directly at the operative.

Countermeasure: Break line-of-sight around corners or bait into dead ends.
MEDUSA THREAT: 5/5

Optical Disintegration Turret

Immobile optical death machine. Any operative stepping into Medusa's cardinal line of sight (up, down, left, right) without solid obstruction is instantly vaporized with zero delay.

Countermeasure: Must be shielded with alloy blocks, trees, or egg shells.
DON MEDUSA THREAT: 5+/5

Patrolling Mobile Gorgon

A mobile Medusa variant that constantly patrols horizontal or vertical rails. Lethal optical rays project perpendicular to its movement axis. Extreme timing and dynamic cover required!

Countermeasure: Pin behind push blocks to restrict its patrol highway.
LEEPER THREAT: 2/5

Kinetic Energy Siphon

Agile bipedal entity that chases Lolo. Does not kill upon contact; instead, touching Lolo causes Leeper to drain kinetic energy, curling into an impenetrable, permanent sleeping boulder!

Tactical Secret: Bait Leeper into a 1-tile corridor to plug enemy firing lanes.
ROCKY THREAT: 3/5

Heavy Kinetic Deflector

A dense armored automaton that marches back and forth. Does not directly deal lethal damage, but violently shoves Lolo backwards on contact, pushing operatives into Medusa rays or water!

Countermeasure: Step aside into alcoves or encase in stasis egg.
SERPENT THREAT: 4/5

Aquatic Sub-Surface Stalker

Lurks submerged underneath water and nanite slime tiles. Surfaces periodically to snap at operatives walking along adjacent liquid shorelines.

Countermeasure: Traverse liquid on egg rafts without lingering on banks.
DOPPELGÄNGER THREAT: 3/5

Holographic Mirror Operative

Simulates and mirrors Lolo's exact movement vectors. Pushes alloy blocks and triggers remote pressure plates. Does not harm Lolo, but serves as a vital cooperative puzzle ally and spatial puzzle obstacle!

Tactical Role: Guide into remote alcoves to weigh down distant switches.
TESLA SENTINEL THREAT: 5/5

High-Voltage EMP Conduit

Generates continuous ionizing lightning arcs along cardinal lines of sight. If its arc strikes water, it electrifies the entire connected pool with high-voltage current! Lethal on contact.

Countermeasure: Push an alloy block or egg into the beam to sever the arc.
MAGNETRON THREAT: 3/5

Gravitational Vector Drone

Levitates in place emitting periodic magnetic distortion pulses along its facing corridor. Pulls or pushes movable alloy blocks 1 tile closer, altering cover configurations dynamically.

Countermeasure: Anchor corridors using heavy titanium or immovable blocks.
PHASE WEAVER THREAT: 2/5

Quantum Teleporting Prism

A non-hostile quantum prism entity that hops across open floor coordinates every few moves. Acts as a dynamic laser shield, intermittently blocking Medusa and Gol sightlines!

Tactical Role: Time your sprints across danger corridors while it shields you.
STEALTH SPECTER THREAT: 4/5

Cloaked Cyber-Stalker

Operates with active adaptive camouflage (nearly 90% transparent). Stalks Lolo silently across the chamber. Revealed when disturbing water/lava ripples, walking on slime footprints, or crossing laser beams!

Countermeasure: Track fluid ripples and slime footprints to pinpoint its path.

CYBER TERRAINS & PHYSICAL PHENOMENA

Water Moats & Thermal Lava

Impassable to foot travel. Dropping push blocks or stasis eggs creates buoyant rafts. Lava features dynamic incandescent heat cracks and fiery ember convection.

Force Floor Conveyors

Vector-propulsion tiles that propel objects and operatives in the arrow direction. Walking against conveyor flow is strictly restricted or causes heavy friction resistance.

Emerald Prism Deflectors

Crystalline blocks that reflect incoming high-energy laser beams at sharp 90-degree angles. Pushable by Lolo to divert beam routing around corners.

Demolition Ordnance (Bombs)

Volatile explosive blocks. Pushing a bomb into cracked masonry or walls causes an immediate high-yield demolition blast, breaching new paths and clearing rubble.

Quantum Singularity Portals

Sub-atomic displacement nodes that instantly warp any traversing entity to the corresponding paired singularity node across the chamber.

Nanite Slime Pools

Bioluminescent green sludge. Traversable on foot, but applies a 50% movement friction drag and emits floating organic spore particles.

Pressure Plates & Laser Security Gates

Dual color-coded logic channels (Cyan & Magenta). Stepping on or weighing down a Pressure Plate disarms the matching high-intensity Laser Gate, clearing safe passage.

Blink / Phase Rhythm Floors

Cycles rhythmically between solid holographic floor (70 ticks) and empty abyss (50 ticks). Watch the perimeter countdown ring! Standing on a blink floor when it de-phases drops the operative into the abyss.

Shatter-Glass Decaying Panels

High-tension crystalline panels. Stepping onto glass the 1st time causes audible structural stress fractures. Stepping on it a 2nd time shatters the tile completely into a water pit!

Weapon Turret Sockets

Heavy titanium circular mounts. Pushing an encased enemy egg onto the socket locks it in place, converting the captive egg into an automated defense cannon firing periodic plasma bolts down its lane!

Cross-System Elemental Synergies
🔥 THERMAL COMBUSTION

Explosions or plasma fire hitting Nanite Sludge ignites the slime into flames, burning it away into clean concrete floor!

⚡ ELECTRIFIED WATER

Tesla Sentinel arcs striking water electrify connected pools with lethal current until severed by a block or egg.

🛶 RAFT HIJACKING

Aggressive chasers (Almas and awakened Skulls) can step onto floating egg rafts to navigate liquid moats!

TACTICAL SECRETS & SPEEDRUNNING PRO TIPS

1. The Stasis Line-of-Sight Shield

Encasement in an egg makes an enemy behave as a solid push block. Position an egg directly in front of a Medusa or active Gol to completely nullify their deadly line-of-sight firing rays while you collect nearby hearts.

2. Double-Shot Despawn Cycling

Firing a second plasma shot destroys the egg. After a brief delay, the enemy respawns back at its original spawn tile. Use this to remove a threatening hostile from an exit doorway and reset it to its far starting location!

3. Corner & Half-Step Sniping

Projectiles travel cleanly down cardinal corridors. By positioning Lolo around a corner or firing just as an enemy enters alignment, you can encase hostiles without exposing yourself to their retaliation.

4. Leeper Funnel Barrier

When a Leeper touches Lolo, it falls asleep forever. Stand in front of an aggressive Alma or Skull lane and let the Leeper touch you; its sleeping body becomes an unbreakable barrier that locks the other enemy away for the entire stage!

5. Zero-Penalty Temporal Undoing (Key Z)

Don't fear experimentation! The temporal rewind drive stores every physical state without score penalty or move count inflation. Test egg placement or raft drifts, then rewind 5 moves back if the timing is tight.
