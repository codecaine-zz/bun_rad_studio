## 1. PROLOGUE & MISSION LORE

In the neon-drenched dystopia of the late 21st century, the **Great Algorithm Collapse** corrupted the planetary cybernetic grid. Autonomous rogue sub-routines seized control of the megastructure fortress known as the **Eggerland Citadel**. At the core of this impenetrable digital labyrinth sits **King Egger**, an omnipotent rogue mainframe entity who has captured and encrypted **Princess Lala**, the sovereign consciousness of Eden City.

You are deployed as **LOLO** (Low-latency Optical Logic Operative), a high-agility tactical cyber-sphere equipped with an omni-directional repulsor chassis and high-energy plasma stasis emitters. Your mission: infiltrate 100 interconnected logic chambers across 5 escalating security sectors, decrypt the memory cores, disarm autonomous sentry algorithms, breach the Citadel Gate, and liberate Lala before the system purge becomes irreversible.

## 2. CORE DIRECTIVES & OPERATIONAL RULES

Every chamber in the Citadel represents a discrete, mathematically verifiable logic test. Operatives must execute the following sequence to clear each chamber:

### A. Collect All Heart Framers (Memory Cores)
- Each chamber contains a fixed count of red glowing **Heart Framers**.
- You must collect every Heart Framer on the board to unlock the central chamber chest.
- **Ammo Batteries:** Specific Heart Framers contain compressed plasma ammo batteries. Collecting them awards **2 Plasma Stasis Blaster shots**. Conserve these shots carefully; many chambers require exact shot counts to solve!

### B. Decrypt the Jewel Chest (Quantum Key Recovery)
- Collecting the final Heart Framer decrypts the central **Jewel Chest**.
- Stepping onto the open chest extracts the chamber's quantum decryption key.
- Retrieving the chest instantly puts all active hostiles on the board into a permanent harmless stasis state and opens the extraction door.

### C. Evacuate via the Warp Gate
- Once the Jewel Chest is secured, navigate to the open chamber door along the upper wall.
- Stepping through the doorway triggers an orbital teleportation beam, logs your time and moves, and advances you to the next sector operation.

## 4. HOSTILE THREAT INTELLIGENCE DOSSIER (BESTIARY)

The Eggerland Citadel employs 9 distinct classes of autonomous cybernetic defense drones. Understand their behaviors, triggers, and countermeasures:

### 1. Snakey (Recon Viper Drone)
- **Threat Level:** 1 / 5 (Harmless)
- **Behavior:** Completely stationary. Never moves or attacks.
- **Tactical Utility:** Prime target for stasis encasement. An encased Snakey becomes a solid push block to shield against Medusas or a floating ferry raft when pushed into liquid moats.
- **Countermeasure:** 1 shot turns into egg raft or cover. 2 shots removes from tight corridors.

### 2. Gol (Thermobaric Plasma Hydra)
- **Threat Level:** 3 / 5 (Dormant until activated)
- **Behavior:** Slumbers with eyes closed during the heart-collection phase. The moment the last Heart Framer is collected, Gol awakens and breathes lethal high-velocity fireballs along cardinal lines of sight.
- **Countermeasure:** Push an alloy block in front of Gol before collecting the final heart, or encase it into an egg just prior to grabbing the last heart.

### 3. Skull (Nanite Cyber Sentry)
- **Threat Level:** 4 / 5 (Lethal Chaser)
- **Behavior:** Appears as a stationary pile of cyber-bones. Awakens the instant the final Heart Framer is hacked, aggressively hunting Lolo using shortest-path tracking.
- **Countermeasure:** Encase in stasis or box in behind push blocks before grabbing the final heart.

### 4. Alma (Rolling Kinetic Juggernaut)
- **Threat Level:** 4 / 5 (Active Patrol)
- **Behavior:** Constantly roams corridors. When Lolo crosses into its aligned sightline, Alma tucks into an armored sphere and accelerates at high speed directly toward the operative.
- **Countermeasure:** Duck behind data trees or round corners. Trapping Alma in dead-end corridors using push blocks neutralizes its charge.

### 5. Medusa (Optical Disintegration Turret)
- **Threat Level:** 5 / 5 (Instant Death)
- **Behavior:** Stationary optical turret. Emits continuous invisible death rays in all 4 cardinal directions (North, South, East, West). Stepping into its unobstructed line of sight results in instantaneous vaporization.
- **Countermeasure:** You MUST block line of sight using solid cover (Data Trees, Alloy Push Blocks, Emerald Prisms, or encased Enemy Eggs).

### 6. Don Medusa (Patrolling Mobile Gorgon)
- **Threat Level:** 5+ / 5 (Extreme Hazard)
- **Behavior:** A mobile variant of Medusa that patrols back and forth along fixed linear tracks. Emits fatal optical rays perpendicular to its travel axis.
- **Countermeasure:** Move across its corridor only when Don Medusa is blocked or facing away, or pin it between blocks to restrict its movement lane.

### 7. Leeper (Kinetic Energy Siphon)
- **Threat Level:** 2 / 5 (Tactical Asset)
- **Behavior:** Agile hopper that pursues Lolo. Does not kill upon contact; instead, touching Lolo causes Leeper to siphon momentum and curl into an unbreakable, permanently sleeping boulder!
- **Tactical Secret:** Intentionally bait Leeper into a 1-tile corridor chokepoint to permanently plug charging Almas or Skulls.

### 8. Rocky (Heavy Kinetic Deflector)
- **Threat Level:** 3 / 5 (Physical Hazard)
- **Behavior:** Armored bouncer that paces corridors. Does not kill on touch, but violently shoves Lolo backward, potentially shoving the operative into water, lava, or Medusa lines of sight.
- **Countermeasure:** Sidestep into alcoves or encase in a stasis egg.

### 9. Serpent (Aquatic Sub-Surface Stalker)
- **Threat Level:** 4 / 5 (Amphibious Threat)
- **Behavior:** Lurks submerged in water or toxic nanite slime. Surfacing periodically, it attacks operatives walking too close to unshielded liquid banks.
- **Countermeasure:** Sail rapidly across moats using egg rafts without lingering near the shoreline.

### 10. Doppelgänger (Mirror Operative)
- **Threat Level:** 3 / 5 (Tactical Clone)
- **Behavior:** Mirrors Lolo's exact moves. Can push alloy blocks and depress remote pressure plates.
- **Countermeasure:** Guide it against walls to offset its alignment, or use it to hold open laser security doors.

### 11. Tesla Sentinel (High-Voltage EMP Conduit)
- **Threat Level:** 5 / 5 (Continuous Lethal Beam)
- **Behavior:** Projects an intense crackling electrical arc in its facing direction. If the beam strikes water, it electrifies the entire connected pool!
- **Countermeasure:** Sever the current by pushing an alloy block, reflector prism, or encased egg into the beam path.

### 12. Magnetron (Gravitational Vector Drone)
- **Threat Level:** 3 / 5 (Dynamic Environmental Shift)
- **Behavior:** Emits periodic magnetic pulses pulling or pushing movable alloy blocks along corridors.
- **Countermeasure:** Block line-of-sight using fixed structures, or anchor your position with heavy titanium blocks.

### 13. Phase Weaver (Quantum Teleporting Prism)
- **Threat Level:** 2 / 5 (Allied Dynamic Shield)
- **Behavior:** Non-hostile quantum entity that teleports across open floor tiles every few moves, providing moving laser cover.
- **Countermeasure:** Dash through Medusa corridors while the Phase Weaver intercepts enemy sightlines.

### 14. Stealth Specter (Cloaked Cyber-Stalker)
- **Threat Level:** 4 / 5 (Invisible Hunter)
- **Behavior:** Stalks Lolo with 90% optical transparency. Instantly revealed upon touching water/lava, walking on slime, or crossing laser beams.
- **Countermeasure:** Track environmental ripples in fluids and glowing footprints in slime.

## 5. CYBER TERRAINS, HAZARDS & PHYSICAL PHENOMENA

- **Concrete & Alloy Floors:** Standard traversable terrain with zero friction drag.
- **Data Trees & Cyber Flora:** Impenetrable natural cover. Completely blocks enemy line of sight (Medusas cannot see through trees) and projectile fire.
- **High Grass / Holographic Foliage:** Soft cover. Operatives and hostiles can traverse grass tiles freely, but plasma blaster shots cannot penetrate through grass.
- **Water Moats:** Liquid barriers impassable on foot. Pushing blocks or stasis eggs into water creates floating bridges or drifting rafts.
- **Molten Magma:** High-temperature liquid moats. Features incandescent heat fissures and buoyant fire embers. Can be sailed upon using egg rafts.
- **Toxic Nanite Sludge (Slime):** Bioluminescent green sludge. Foot traversal is permitted, but applies a 50% movement drag penalty and emits floating bio-spores.
- **Force Floor Conveyors:** Directional acceleration tiles (Up, Down, Left, Right). Propels objects and operatives in the marked direction. Walking against conveyor flow requires extreme counter-thrust.
- **Emerald Laser Prisms:** Reflective optical blocks that redirect laser beams at sharp 90-degree angles. Pushable by Lolo.
- **Demolition Ordnance (Bombs):** Pushable explosive charges. Pushing a bomb into cracked masonry detonates the ordnance, vaporizing adjacent wall barriers.
- **Quantum Singularity Portals:** Paired displacement nodes that translocate any entering entity across the chamber instantly.
- **Pressure Plates & Laser Security Gates (Cyan & Magenta):** Dual-channel security grid. Depressing a pressure plate with Lolo, an alloy block, a sleeping Leeper, or an encased egg disarms the corresponding high-intensity laser gate.
- **Phase / Blink Rhythm Floors:** Holographic flooring that phases between solid ground (70 ticks) and bottomless abyss (50 ticks). Watch the countdown ring; de-phased tiles drop operatives into the abyss!
- **Shatter-Glass Decaying Panels:** Fragile crystalline floor panels. Cracks on the 1st step with audible stress fracturing; collapses into a water pit on the 2nd step, enforcing strict one-way routes.
- **Weapon Turret Sockets:** Heavy titanium mounts. Pushing an encased enemy egg onto the socket clamps it in place, converting the captive egg into an automated defensive cannon firing periodic plasma bolts!

### Cross-System Elemental Synergies:
- **Thermal Slime Combustion:** Nitro bomb explosions or plasma fire ignite Nanite Sludge, vaporizing it into clean floor.
- **Electrified Water Moats:** Tesla Sentinel arcs striking liquid electrify the water grid until severed.
- **Enemy Raft Hijacking:** Almas and awakened Skulls can step onto floating egg rafts to navigate liquid moats.

## 9. TACTICAL SECRETS & SPEEDRUNNING PRO TIPS

1. **The Stasis Line-of-Sight Shield:** An encased enemy egg is completely solid. Place an egg directly in front of a Medusa or Gol to nullify its line of fire while you gather surrounding hearts.
2. **Double-Shot Despawn Cycling:** Firing a second shot destroys an egg. The enemy respawns at its initial starting position after a brief cooldown. Use this to permanently clear a doorway and push the enemy back to the other side of the room.
3. **Corner & Half-Step Sniping:** Projectiles travel down cardinal axes. You can fire around corners or step into an enemy's line of sight for a fraction of a second to fire a shot before their attack animation can trigger.
4. **Leeper Chokepoint Wall:** Bait a Leeper to touch you inside a 1-tile gap. Its permanent sleeping shell forms an impenetrable wall that traps charging Almas and Skulls behind it forever.
5. **Zero-Penalty Temporal Undoing (Key Z):** Test risky moves freely! The temporal rewind drive stores your entire physical trajectory without move-count penalties or score loss.
