👾 CyberInvaders: StarSwarm — Defender Manual

Fuses 1978 Space Invaders marching formations & destructible plasma bunkers with 1981 Galaga dive-bomber swoops and Twin-Fighter upgrades.

  • ← / → or A / D or Mouse — Strafe Defender Fighter
  • Space or Left Click — Fire Plasma Blasters
  • E — Unleash Overcharge Wave Cannon when Combo Meter reaches 100% (vaporizes an entire vertical column of invaders!)
  • 5×10 StarSwarm Fleet: Commanders (Top Row, 40 pts), Cruisers (Middle Rows, 20 pts), and Grunts (Bottom Rows, 10 pts) accelerate as their ranks thin and launch Galaga-style dive-bomber swoops!
  • 4 Destructible Plasma Bunkers: Absorb alien fire and erode chunk-by-chunk on impact.
  • Red Mystery UFO: Flies across the top for 300 bonus points and drops Twin-Fighter Dual-Blaster or Bunker Shield Repair pods!
