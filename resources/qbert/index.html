🟧 CYBERCUBE HOPPER — HOW TO PLAY

Hop diagonally across all 28 isometric cubes of the 7-row Cyber-Pyramid to flip every top face to the target neon color!

  • Diagonal Keys: Use Q (Up-Left), W (Up-Right), A (Down-Left), S (Down-Right), Arrow Keys, or click any adjacent cube directly.
  • Spinning Escape Discs: Hop off the outer edge onto a floating disc to ride the elevator beam back to the top apex. If Coily the Snake is right behind you, he leaps into the void for +500 points!
  • Green Chrono-Orbs: Catch them to freeze all hazards for 5 seconds.
