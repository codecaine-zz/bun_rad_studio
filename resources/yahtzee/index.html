📖 OFFICIAL YACHT DICE RULES & STRATEGY

🎯 OBJECT OF THE GAME

Roll 5 dice to score points across 13 combinations over 13 rounds. The player with the highest Grand Total wins.

🎲 ROLLING MECHANICS

On each turn, you may roll the dice up to 3 times. After Roll 1 or Roll 2, you may hold ("keep") any number of dice and re-roll the remaining unkept dice. After Roll 3, or earlier if satisfied, you must choose an open category on your score card to record a score.

🏆 UPPER SECTION & 35-POINT BONUS

In the Upper Section (Aces through Sixes), you only count the dice matching that category number. If the total of your six Upper Section scores reaches 63 or more (the equivalent of three of each number), you earn a valuable +35 point Bonus!

✨ YACHT BONUS & JOKER RULE

A Yacht is 5 of a kind and scores 50 points. If you roll an additional Yacht and your Yacht box already contains 50 points, you earn an extra +100 point Yacht Bonus! You may then use this wild Yacht as a Joker in the appropriate upper or lower section box (scoring Full House, Straight, etc.).

💡 PRO STRATEGY TIPS

  • Focus on getting at least three 4s, 5s, and 6s in the upper section to secure the +35 bonus.
  • Never scratch Chance early; keep it as a safety net when a difficult lower combination misses.
  • Small Straights (30 pts) are easier to hit than 4 of a Kind; look out for 1-2-3-4, 2-3-4-5, and 3-4-5-6 sequences.
