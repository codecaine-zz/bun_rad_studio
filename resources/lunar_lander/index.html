🚀 CyberLander Flight Manual
Pilot your vector excursion module to a precision touchdown on any glowing horizontal Landing Pad (`1x`, `2x`, `3x`, or narrow canyon `5x`). Landing on a **FUEL DEPOT** pad replenishes propellant reserves for extended missions!

Safe Touchdown Parameters:
• Vertical Descent (`VY`): < 2.20 m/s
• Horizontal Drift (`VX`): < 1.60 m/s
• Attitude Tilt (`DEG`): Within ±10° of vertical
