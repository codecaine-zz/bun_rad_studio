📖 Modern Touch-Typing Methodology & Ergonomics

1. Tactile Home Row Anchors

Rest your Left Index on F and Right Index on J—feel the physical tactile ridges without looking down. Curve your remaining fingers over A S D and K L ;. After every upper or lower row reach, snap that finger immediately back to its home key.

2. Floating Wrists & Posture

Sit with elbows at a 90°–100° open angle. Keep your wrists straight and hovering slightly during active bursts rather than planting heavy weight on the desk edge, preventing carpal tunnel compression and ulnar deviation.

3. Opposite-Hand Shift Rule

When typing a capital letter or shifted symbol, never press Shift and the letter with the same hand. If the target key is typed with your Left Hand, hold Right Shift with your Right Pinky (and vice versa).

4. Rhythm & Look-Ahead Vision

Elite 120+ WPM typists do not rush fast keys and stall on hard keys—they type with a steady metronomic cadence while keeping their eyes 1 to 2 words ahead of the active caret. Accuracy above 97% builds automatic muscle memory.
