🐛 CYBERCENTIPEDE BLASTER — BRIEFING

Pilot your Cyber-Blaster inside the lower 25% defense zone and vaporize the descending multi-segment Centipede!

  • Chain Splitting: Shooting any midsection segment turns that cell into a Cyber-Spore and splits the Centipede into two independent chains with their own heads!
  • Bouncing Spider: Zig-zags through the player zone—shoot it up close for up to 900 points!
  • Flea & Scorpion: Fleas drop new spores vertically; Scorpions poison spores so the Centipede dives straight down!
