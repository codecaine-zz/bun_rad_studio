💥 SCORCHED SKYLINE — BALLISTICS MANUAL

Inspired by MS-DOS QBasic GORILLAS.BAS (1991) and Scorched Earth (1991)! Command your rooftop Cyber-Mech across a pixel-destructible skyscraper skyline.

  • Angle (`↑` / `↓`) & Velocity (`←` / `→`): Dial in your ballistic arc while compensating for the live Wind Vector at the top of the screen.
  • Pixel-Destructible Buildings: Every explosion carves circular craters directly through skyscrapers so you can tunnel shots through buildings!
  • 5 Tactical Weapons (`1`–`5`): Fire standard Plasma Shells, splitting 3-Way MIRVs, chaotic 5-Burst Funky Bombs, deep-cratering Bunker Busters, or activate Shield + Trajectory Tracer!
