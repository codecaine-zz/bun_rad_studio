🎯 TACTICAL INTERCEPTOR BRIEFING

Defend the 6 neon Cyber-Cities along the sector horizon from incoming orbital ICBMs, MIRV splitters, Orbital Bombers, and Smart Evader Warheads.

  • Mouse Click / Space: Fire interceptor from the nearest armed battery toward the target reticle.
  • A / 1: Fire specifically from Alpha Battery (Left).
  • S / 2: Fire specifically from Beta Hypersonic Battery (Center — 1.65× velocity!).
  • D / 3: Fire specifically from Gamma Battery (Right).
  • Arrow Keys: Move target crosshair with keyboard.
  • Chain Reactions: Enemy warheads caught inside a thermonuclear plasma sphere detonate into secondary blast spheres!
  • Bonus City: Every 10,000 pts rebuilds 1 destroyed Cyber-City at wave end.
