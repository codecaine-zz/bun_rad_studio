🎈 Codecaine CyberBalloon Fight — Pilot Flight Manual

🕹️ Player 1 & Player 2 Controls

• P1 Flap / Bank: W / Space to Flap, A / D to Bank

• P1 Pump / Dive: S or R on platform to re-inflate 2nd balloon; S in midair for Dive-Stomp

• P1 Afterburner & Dart: Shift Air-Dash, F Feather Dart

• P2 (Co-Op & Versus): Up Arrow to Flap, Left / Right to Bank, Down Arrow to Pump/Dive, Enter Dash

⚡ 1985 NES + Modern Mechanics

• Two-Step Knockdown: Pop an enemy's balloon from above so they drop via Parachute, then strike their parachute or kick them while pumping on a platform!

• Enemy Tier Upgrades: If a landed enemy finishes pumping, their balloon upgrades from Pink → Green → Red (faster & more aggressive)!

• Hazards: Watch out for Storm Cloud Lightning Sparks, spinning Flippers, and the Giant Piranha lurking in the water!
