☄️ VoidVector Asteroids Deluxe — Pilot Briefing

Navigate deep-space asteroid fields with true Newtonian inertia, glowing vector line-segment shatters, and Hunter-Seeker UFO saucers.

  • ← / → or A / D — Rotate Vector Interceptor
  • ↑ or W — Fire Newtonian Main Thrusters
  • Space or Left Click — Fire Plasma Cannon
  • Shift or S — Hold Rechargeable Energy Shield (absorbs impacts & boulders!)
  • H or E — Emergency Hyperspace Warp to random sector coordinates
  • Asteroid Shatter Chain: Large boulders (20 pts) split into 2 Medium (50 pts) → 2 Small (100 pts).
  • Hunter-Seeker UFOs: Large Saucers (200 pts) & Small Sharpshooter Saucers (1,000 pts) drop Weapon Power-Up Orbs: Twin Spread Shot, Rapid Pulse, and Rear Tailgun!
