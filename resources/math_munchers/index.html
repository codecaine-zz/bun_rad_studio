📖 CyberMunch Math Deluxe — 8-Mode & 6-Tier Guide

6-Tier Beginner-to-Advanced Math Curriculum

Select any curriculum tier in the top bar (or use Adaptive Auto): 🌱 Sprout (Counting, Even/Odd, Sums ≤ 20), 🧭 Explorer (Multiples, Factors, Primes, Fractions), 📐 Scholar (Integers, Exponents, Order of Ops, GCD/LCM), ⚡ Honors (Linear Equations, Quadratics, Radicals, Logs), ∫ AP Calculus (Unit Circle Trig, Derivatives, Integrals, Limits), and 💻 CS Hacker (Binary, Hexadecimal, Bitwise Ops, Modulo).

8 Game Modes & Tactical Gadgets

Switch anytime between 1. Classic, 2. Fractions & %, 3. Super Munchers, 4. Algebra Quest, 5. Trig & Calc, 6. CS Hacker, 7. Mega-Troggle Boss Raid, and 8. 2P Math Squad (P1 WASD+Space, P2 Arrows+Enter). Use ❄️ Freeze [F], 💡 Tutor [H], and ⚡ Zap Troggle [Q]—every 5-Munch Streak grants a bonus gadget charge!

The 5 Classic Glitcher Species

👹 Reggie (Red)
Marches straight across a single row or column from edge to edge.
😰 Bashful (Blue)
Wanders the grid, but gets scared and flees if your Muncher faces it head-on!
😋 Helper (Gold)
Eats numbers off the board as it walks! Helpful when it eats wrong answers.
🔧 Worker / Glitcher (Purple)
Plants new numbers or changes existing numbers on the squares it crosses!
🤓 Smarty (Green Cyclops)
The smartest Troggle—actively stalks your Muncher across the board!
💥 Troggle-Eat-Troggle!
Lure two Troggles onto the same square and one will eat the other!

Scoring, Hints & Extra Lives

Correct Munch: +100 pts × Streak. Press H (or click 💡 Hint) to highlight a correct number. Earn a Bonus Muncher Life every 5,000 points!
