📖 Muncher Protocol Manual

Objective

You control the Cyber Muncher. Move across the 6×5 grid and chomp all cells that satisfy the current Mathematical Directive. When all correct numbers are cleared, you advance to the next level!

Troggle Bestiary

👾 Reggie (Purple)
Standard patrol troggle. Wanders the grid looking to squash the Muncher!
👹 Basher (Red)
Aggressive lane rusher. Charges swiftly across columns and rows.
🤖 Glitcher (Yellow)
Matrix disruptor. Changes numbers on tiles it walks over!
🛡️ Safe Pods
Freeze Pods stop troggles for 6s; Zappers banish them; Shields absorb 1 hit!

Scoring & Combos

Correct Munch: +100 pts × Streak Multiplier (up to 5x). Munching an incorrect number costs 1 life and resets your combo. Clear entire board for a +1,000 pts Level Clear Bonus!
