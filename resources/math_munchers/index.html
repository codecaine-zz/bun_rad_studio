📖 Original MECC Troggle & Muncher Guide

How to Play

Guide the green Muncher across the 6×5 grid and press SPACE to chomp numbers that match the rule at the top of the screen. Watch for flashing TROGGLE! arrows on the grid border before a Troggle enters!

The 5 Original MECC Troggle Species

👹 Reggie (Red)
Marches straight across a single row or column from edge to edge.
😰 Bashful (Blue)
Wanders the grid, but gets scared and flees if your Muncher faces it head-on!
😋 Helper (Gold)
Eats numbers off the board as it walks! Helpful when it eats wrong answers.
🔧 Worker / Glitcher (Purple)
Plants new numbers or changes existing numbers on the squares it crosses!
🤓 Smarty (Green Cyclops)
The smartest Troggle—actively stalks your Muncher across the board!
💥 Troggle-Eat-Troggle!
Lure two Troggles onto the same square and one will eat the other!

Scoring, Hints & Extra Lives

Correct Munch: +100 pts × Streak. Press H (or click 💡 Hint) to highlight a correct number. Earn a Bonus Muncher Life every 5,000 points!
