💣 CyberBomber Grid Arena — Lifetime DX Manual

Inspired by Super Bomberman R, Saturn Bomberman, and Bomberman '94, featuring 8 game modes, 6 interactive biomes, Cyber-Louie Kangaroo mounts, and Titan Bosses.

  • 8 Game Modes: 50-Stage Campaign (with Pontan portal defense & Bosses every 5th stage), 4-Bomber Battle Royale (with Sudden Death & Revenge Carts), Endless Roguelike Dungeon (with 3-Card Perk Drafts), Titan Mech Boss Colosseum, Grand Prix Crown Heist, Skull & Plague Survival, 2P Co-Op, and 2P Versus.
  • Iconic Arsenal: Bomb Up (💣), Fire Up (🔥), Full Fire (🌞), Speed Skates (👟), Bomb Kick (👟💥, stop with X), Boxing/Power Glove (🥊 E), Remote Detonator (📡 Q), Pierce Bomb (💠), Line Bomb (🧨 C), Vest Shield (🛡️), and 4 Cyber-Louie Kangaroo Mounts (🦘 V) hatched from Spotted Eggs!
  • 6 Arena Biomes: Cyber Grid, Conveyor Factory (moving belts), Warp Portal Lab (teleporters), Cryo Slippery Ice, Magma Geyser Core (periodic vents), and Random Biome.
