⚪ CyberReversi & Checkers AI Manual
1. CyberReversi (Othello): Place discs to outflank opponent lines in any of 8 directions. Valid move indicators display the exact number of discs each move will flip (`+1`, `+3`, etc.). Corners carry highest strategic weight!

2. CyberCheckers (Draughts): Move diagonally on dark squares. Jump opponent pieces to capture (multi-jump chains are automatic when available). Reach the far back rank to crown a **King (`👑`)** that moves & jumps both forward and backward!
