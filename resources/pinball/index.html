🛸 CyberCadet Pinball 2099 — Flight Manual

Inspired by classic Space Cadet pinball, engineered with a 60fps 8x sub-stepped 2D vector physics engine.

  • Z / ← / A — Activate Left Flipper
  • / / → / D — Activate Right Flipper
  • Space or ↓ — Hold & release Spring Plunger to launch ball
  • X — Tactical Table Nudge (careful: excessive nudging causes a TILT warning!)
  • P — Pause / Resume • R — New Game • M — Mute Synth
  • 3 Drop-Target Mission Lights: Light all 3 yellow cyber-targets on the left bank to supercharge the Wormhole & re-arm the Left Outlane Kickback!
  • Wormhole Black Hole Well: Captures the ball inside the upper-left vortex, awards a massive Mission Bonus, and launches 2-Ball Multiball!
