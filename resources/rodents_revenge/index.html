How to Play Rodent's Revenge

🐭
The Agile Mouse: Move 8-directionally with QWEASDZC, Arrow Keys, or touch swipe.
🟩
Push Trains of Blocks: Push single blocks or connected trains of blocks in 4 cardinal directions, provided the far end is empty or hazard-clearing.
🧱
Unmovable Stone Blocks: Solid granite masonry obstacles from the 1991 classic! They cannot be pushed and create tactical choke points to box in cats.
🪤
Mousetraps & Pocket Traps: Watch your step! Walking into an armed trap snaps instantly. Push blocks onto traps to disarm them safely! You can also bait chasing cats into mousetraps for instant revenge cheese, or deploy a pocket trap with T!
🕳️
Sinkholes: Dangerous floor pits! If you step in, you get stuck—tap arrow keys rapidly to wiggle free before cats reach you! Push a block into a sinkhole to permanently fill and pave it. Normal cats fall in and get eliminated!
🚪
Mouse Holes (Burrows): Subterranean express tunnels! Step into any arched mouse hole to instantly teleport to the linked hole across the board with temporary shadow stealth. Cats are too fat to fit inside!
⏰
Ticking Clock & Reinforcements: Authentic 1991 pressure! Finish the level before the reinforcement clock runs out, or an extra cat will spawn into the arena!
🧀
Cheese Varieties: Classic Cheddar (+Score), Golden Swiss (+Powerups), Spicy Pepper Jack (Speed Dash burst!), and Stinky Blue Cheese (scares all cats away for 5 seconds!).
💡 Pro Strategy: Corner cats into 1x1 wall pockets. Use Trap (T) behind you while running, Freeze (F) when swarmed, or dive into a Mouse Hole to escape!
