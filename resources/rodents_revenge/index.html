How to Play Rodent's Revenge

🐭
The Agile Mouse: Move 8-directionally with QWEASDZC, Arrow Keys, or touch swipe.
🟩
Push Long Trains of Blocks: You can push multiple connected blocks at once, as long as there is an empty tile at the far end!
🐱
Box in Every Cat: Trap cats so they have zero escape squares. They instantly turn into delicious cheese wheels!
👑
Special Archetypes: Watch out for the giant King Fat Cat Boss, sneaky Ninja Siamese that jump gaps, and sleepy orange tabbies!
⏳
Cheese Hardening: Eat the cheese fast! If left unattended, it will petrify into an impassable solid block.
💡 Pro Strategy: Corner cats into 1x1 pockets against the wall. Use Freeze (F) when swarmed or Dynamite (B) if you trap yourself.
